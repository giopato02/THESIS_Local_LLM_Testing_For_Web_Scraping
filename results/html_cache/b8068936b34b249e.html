network

Our international innovation network for the development of recyclable clothing that is produced without sewing and gluing consists of partners from the countries

     Austria,
    Great Britain and
    Germany.

In the network, the entire future value chain is represented by the very interdisciplinary partners with the areas of competence that are necessary for the time being and thus an intensive knowledge and demand transfer is possible. The different areas of competence of the partners and their experience in different economic sectors lead to the expectation of considerable synergy effects and great opportunities for implementation. In addition, by involving a number of supporters and multipliers, this structure enables further inflow of competence, accelerated introduction of developed innovations (products, processes, etc.) almost without loss of time and taking into account concrete national/regional needs.

 

The network partners see large sales markets for the new products and services, also due to the internationality of the network, and thus new sales fields and future security for their own companies and jobs.

The network is to develop into a competence carrier for the topic area of sprayed clothing (initially in the area of motorcycle protective clothing) and is also to be an international contact partner for problems relating to the above-mentioned issue.
