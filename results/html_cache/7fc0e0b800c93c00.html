  • ALTER Technology, Ramonville Saint Agne, France

    ECPE Contact: 
    Laurent Guillot

    Website

    A leading OSAT provider, ALTER delivers comprehensive backend solutions, including wafer probing, assembly, packaging, reliability testing, technology analysis, and radiation testing and engineering.
    With expertise in complex materials and high-reliability sectors such as aerospace, defense, automotive, medical, and power technologies, ALTER specializes in solutions for harsh environments, integrating advanced testing and sustainability-driven practices to foster innovation, ensure reliability, and add value for global customers.

    • Electrical & Reliability Testing can be conducted at various stages of the manufacturing process, including the die, wafer, package, and module levels. Each of these stages has its own set of testing techniques and objectives based on the device datasheet and according to different standards such as MIL Std., JEDEC, AECQ, ESA Std, Nasa Std and specific customer requirements.
    • Technology & Failure Analysis Lab can support Physical and chemical control of the device quality, light or deep dive physical analysis for die and assembly process characterization, failure analysis to try to identify root cause of the failure mode and mechanism, monitoring and control of PCB/PCBA process following IPC, MIL, IEC, ECSS, and JEDEC and sample preparation for physical analysis, defect location, FIB (Focus Ion Beam) circuit edit, radiation and laser tests.
    • Radiation engineering for aerospace focuses on designing and simulating shielding solutions for electronic components to withstand radiation in harsh environments. This includes addressing single event effects (SEE) in spacecraft, caused by high-energy particles disrupting circuits. By modeling radiation exposure and predicting component behavior, we provide simulation data to help customers implement effective shielding and mitigation strategies, ensuring reliable performance in high-radiation conditions
    • Space Radiation environment impact significantly the performances of electronic devices. Cumulative effects such as Total Ionizing Dose (TID) and Total Non-Ionizing Dose (TNID) induce electrical parameters to drift out of their expected ranges (as defined in datasheets). Additional exposure to radiation may lead to parts loss of functionality.
    • Parts procurement through IDM (Integrated Die Manufacturer) and official distributors partners or in partnership with Alter Technology France group with traceability and testing services
